Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go. If you no longer wish to use LiveAccess, you can delete your account in the app, or ask us to delete it. Once your account is closed, it cannot be reopened.

How to delete your account

Delete your account in the app under Profile → Delete Account.

If TPS or your organisation manages your account, contact support through the Contact us form for help with deletion.

Contact us
This opens our Contact us form.

What Happens Next?

  • Account closed: Once your account is closed, it cannot be reopened. Requests made through the Contact us form require processing.
  • Records we keep: Some records are kept to meet TPS's business and legal obligations, as described in our Privacy Policy. These include chats, call history, recordings and voicemail shared with others, plus Firebase analytics. To ask for specific chats, recordings or voicemail to be deleted, email liveaccess@tps.ltd.
Deleting your account can't be undone. Your account can't be reopened once it is closed.
