About

Built by the people behind your process lines.

LiveAccess is made by TPS, an equipment and process integrator for the dairy, food and beverage industries. TPS designs and builds high shear mixers, liquid processing lines and spray drying solutions.

Our engineers know these machines inside out. We built LiveAccess so that knowledge is with the people running them, on the phone already in their pocket.

LiveAccess brings TPS engineering knowledge and support closer to the people running process lines.
