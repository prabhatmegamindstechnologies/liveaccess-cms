Evaporator fouling and falling capacity: causes and checks

When an evaporator gives lower concentrate solids or needs more steam as the run goes on, fouling on the heating surface is the usual cause. Other faults can look the same, so check them before you shorten the run.

By LiveAccess team, TPS · Published 29 October 2026 · Evaporation

Safety first. Steam and hot concentrate cause severe burns. Isolate steam and product, release vacuum or pressure, and let the plant cool before you open inspection covers.

Symptoms

  • Concentrate solids falling during the run at the same feed rate.
  • Steam pressure or temperature difference rising to hold the same output.
  • Burnt deposits or brown spots on the tubes after the run.
  • Shorter time between cleanings than before.

Likely causes

  • Product fouling. Proteins and minerals deposit on hot surfaces. The rate rises with temperature, preheating conditions and product quality.
  • Poor liquid distribution. A blocked or uneven distributor leaves tubes partly dry. Dry areas burn product on fast.
  • Vacuum and venting. Air leaks or poorly vented non-condensable gases reduce heat transfer and change boiling temperatures.
  • Steam side. Wet steam, failed steam traps or condensate that does not drain take heating surface out of use.
  • Incomplete cleaning. Deposits left after CIP give the next run a fouled start.

What to check, in order

  1. Compare feed rate, feed temperature and concentrate solids with a good run at the same point in time.
  2. Check vacuum level and condenser cooling water against your service documentation.
  3. Check steam traps and condensate drainage.
  4. After the run, inspect the distributor and the top of the tubes for blockage and uneven wetting.
  5. Review the last CIP: chemical strength, temperature, time and flow.
  6. Ask whether raw material or preheat conditions changed.

When to call a TPS engineer

Call a TPS engineer if capacity does not recover after a full CIP, if you find burnt deposits in a regular pattern, or before you change the run length or cleaning programme.
