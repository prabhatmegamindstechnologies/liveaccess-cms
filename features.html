Features

Practical tools for everyday plant support.

Find information, get guidance and connect with support, right where you work.

Plain-language search

Ask the way you would ask a colleague, in your preferred language. Provide equipment details when needed to identify the right information.

Camera search

Photograph a nameplate, part or HMI screen and search with the picture.

Voice in, voice out

Speak your question and tap Listen to hear the answer read aloud.

Safety first

Where supporting documents are available, open them to review the guidance. Always confirm safety-critical steps against your site procedures or with a TPS engineer.

Follow-up questions

Keep asking in the same thread. Log in to view your saved search and conversation history in the app.

Authorised plant information

For TPS customers

Browse your authorised equipment records, drawings and specifications directly in the app. Access is granted by TPS.

Video call a TPS engineer

Log in to show the problem live to a TPS engineer through the app. Engineers answer during their working hours. Video calls include background noise cancellation. Either participant can record a call, and both are notified on screen. If nobody answers, leave a voicemail and they can call you back in the app. Your calls, recording and chats stay in your call history for future review.

Chat or call an expert

Find experts by process area, including high-shear mixing, liquid evaporation, spray drying, powder handling, automation and more, and chat or call them in the app. Your chats and calls stay in your call history for later.

Who it's for

For the people who keep the line running.

Process engineers

Troubleshoot complex equipment and tune production lines with technical advice in front of you.

Maintenance teams

Daily upkeep, planned maintenance and emergency repairs, with technical guidance at the machine.

Production teams

Practical guidance for day-to-day production tasks and operating questions.
