About LiveAccess

What is LiveAccess?

LiveAccess 24/7 is a TPS service app that helps plant teams find equipment information, get practical guidance and connect with TPS expertise. Ask in your own words, use a photo or speak your question. Authorised TPS customers can also access information for their own plant.

Who makes LiveAccess?

LiveAccess is made by TPS, an equipment and process integrator for the dairy, food and beverage industries.

Who is LiveAccess for?

Process engineers, maintenance teams and production teams in dairy, food and beverage plants, especially those running high-shear mixing, liquid processing, evaporation, spray drying and powder handling equipment.

Which devices does LiveAccess run on?

iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets. Download it from the App Store or Google Play.

Using the app

Where is the fastest place to get an answer?

In the app. Download LiveAccess, ask your question by text, photo or voice, and log in to video-call a TPS engineer if you need more help.

Do I need an account?

Not to search. You can search as a guest. Logging in is required to video-call a TPS engineer, and lets you save your search history and continue conversations later.

Where do the answers come from, and can I trust them?

LiveAccess provides technical guidance based on available equipment information and technical knowledge. Authorised customers can also use their project documents. Check safety-critical instructions against your site procedures or with a TPS engineer.

How does the AI search work, and what happens to my questions?

LiveAccess uses AI to read your question and the documentation you are permitted to access, and to write the answer. Your question, the earlier messages in the same conversation, any photo you attach and relevant parts of that documentation are processed to produce the answer. Voice input is turned into text so it can be answered. If an answer cannot be found in the documentation, the AI may answer from general knowledge or public web sources. Answers may be inaccurate or out of date, so verify critical information before acting on it. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I search with a photo or my voice?

Yes. Take or attach a photo of a nameplate, part or screen, or hold the mic and speak. Tap Listen to hear any answer read aloud.

Access & support

Is LiveAccess free?

Yes. LiveAccess is free to download and use, with no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Search general technical information as a guest. Create a free LiveAccess account. Log in to view your saved search and conversation history in the app. Log in to message TPS engineers and experts in the app. You can also video-call a TPS engineer and connect with experts through the TPS Expert Network.

TPS project customers can also search their own plant's documents. TPS grants this access to each person, project by project. To request access or other TPS services, use our Contact us form.

How do I talk to a TPS engineer?

Log in, then tap the video icon to call a TPS engineer through the app. Engineers answer during their working hours, so a call may not be answered immediately. If nobody answers, you can leave a voicemail, and the engineer can call you back in the app. Log in to message TPS engineers and experts in the app.

How do I delete my account?

Delete your account in the app under Profile → Delete Account. If TPS or your organisation manages your account, contact support through the Contact us form for help with deletion. Full steps are on the Delete Your Account page.

Still have a question?

Ask it in the app. If you need more help, log in to contact a TPS engineer in the app. For general enquiries, use our Contact us form.
