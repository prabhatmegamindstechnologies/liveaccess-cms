Get the LiveAccess app.

Free to download for iPhone, iPad and Android. Access equipment information, get practical guidance and connect with TPS support, all from your phone.

  • Ask by text, photo or voice
  • Log in to message TPS engineers and experts in the app. You can also video-call a TPS engineer through the app.
  • Search general technical information as a guest, no account needed.
  • Create a free LiveAccess account to continue conversations and connect with TPS engineers and experts. Log in to view your saved search and conversation history in the app. Access to private plant documents requires separate approval from TPS.
