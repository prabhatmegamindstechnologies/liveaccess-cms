## Request
Replace the coming-soon page at `/la-studio/areas` with a working editor for the process area articles.

## What exists today (checked 2026-11-03)
- 8 areas in `articles` (type `area`, published, `published` copy set by migration 0003): applications, automation,
  evaporation, high-shear-mixing, ingredients, liquid-processing, powder-handling, spray-drying.
- Area page (`area-*.html`, ~9 KB): crumbs (Home / Process areas / name), eyebrow "Process area", `h1` = name,
  `p.lede` = summary (no byline or date), `article.article` blocks (headings, lists, paragraph, disclaimer note),
  then a "Troubleshooting guides / Guides for <name>." band listing that area's guides (cards), and a CTA band
  "Questions about <name lower-case>?" with store badges.
- The 8 areas mirror the app's Expert Network (the site shows "8 process areas in the Expert Network" with chips on
  home/features). Guides point to an area (`area_slug`), and their byline links to `area-<slug>.html`.
- Guides editor (`/guides`) already has the block editor, auto-save, preview, partial validation and
  `articles.published`; areas can reuse all of it.

More from the pages:
- `<title>` "<Name>: process area | LiveAccess"; `og:title` "<Name> | LiveAccess"; JSON-LD is only a BreadcrumbList.
- The guides band shows one card per guide of that area: `<a class="feat feat-link" href="guide-…">` + "Guide" +
  title + the **first sentence** of the guide's summary. Areas with no guides have **no band at all**
  (applications, automation, ingredients, powder-handling). Card order is hand-made (not alphabetical, not by id).
