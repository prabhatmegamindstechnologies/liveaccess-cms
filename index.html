For managers

What plant and maintenance managers need to know.

Access for your team

Anyone can search as a guest. Logging in lets your people save their search history and video-call a TPS engineer. TPS can give authorised customers access to their own plant's equipment records, drawings and project documents.

Setup

Your team downloads the free app from the App Store or Google Play and can start asking questions straight away. For access to your plant's own documents, contact TPS.

Data handling

Chat message text is encrypted on the device before it leaves the app. Attachments, recordings and voicemail are kept in private cloud storage, reached only through short-lived, access-controlled links. TPS does not sell personal data, and anyone can delete their account in the app.

Cost

LiveAccess is free to download and use, with no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Full details are in our Privacy Policy. Questions about access for your team? Contact us.
