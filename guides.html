Troubleshooting guides

Common plant problems, checked step by step.

Practical checks for common faults in dairy, food and beverage plants. Each guide lists symptoms, likely causes, what to check first and when to call a TPS engineer.

High shear mixing

High shear mixer seal leaks: causes and checks

A leaking shaft seal on a high shear mixer is one of the most common maintenance calls.

 Spray drying

Spray dryer powder build-up: causes and checks

Deposits on the chamber wall, cone or ducts reduce capacity, change powder quality and raise the fire risk.

 Evaporation

Evaporator fouling and falling capacity: causes and checks

When an evaporator gives lower concentrate solids or needs more steam as the run goes on, fouling on the heating surface is the usual cause.

 High shear mixing

Lumps and undissolved powder after mixing: causes and checks

Lumps, often called fish-eyes, form when powder is wetted on the outside before it is dispersed.

 Liquid processing

Plate heat exchanger leaks and mixing of media: what to check

A plate heat exchanger can leak to the outside, or leak inside so that product and service media mix.

 Liquid processing

Centrifugal pump noise and low flow: cavitation checks

A centrifugal pump that sounds like gravel, delivers less than usual or wears its impeller fast is often cavitating.
